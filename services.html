WHAT WE OFFER

Physiotherapy Services Designed Around Your Needs

Each treatment plan is tailored according to your condition, mobility, daily activities and recovery goals.

Back Pain Physiotherapy

Personalized assessment and treatment to help reduce back pain, improve mobility, strengthen supporting muscles and support safer movement during daily activities.

Knee Pain Physiotherapy

Targeted exercises and rehabilitation to improve knee mobility, strength, stability and confidence during walking and everyday activities.

Shoulder Pain Physiotherapy

Individualized treatment to improve shoulder movement, reduce discomfort, restore strength and support better function during daily activities.

Geriatric Physiotherapy

Gentle, safe and personalized physiotherapy for older adults to support mobility, strength, balance, independence and confidence at home.

Post-Operative Rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation after surgery to support safe recovery, restore movement, improve strength and help patients return gradually to daily activities.

Balance & Mobility Training

Personalized exercises to improve balance, coordination, walking ability and confidence, while supporting safer movement and reducing the risk of falls.
